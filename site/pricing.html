How billing works

  • Monthly plans are billed monthly. Annual billing gives two months free.
  • The setup fee covers moving your opening records, setting up your chart of accounts, and training your team.
  • Prices are shown in US dollars. Your local currency is shown on your written quote.
  • Nothing is charged until you have seen the product and agreed to the written terms.
  • Cancellation and data export when you leave are set out in those terms.
