Seven questions to ask before you choose an ERP

By the QAVRIL LABS team · Published 2026-11-02

  1. Can we start with the modules we need today and add others later? Growth should not mean a new system.
  2. Does it handle the currencies and tax rules we use? Ask for an example from your own country.
  3. Who can see what? Check that each person sees only the records their role allows.
  4. What happens when the internet drops? Ask whether the till and stock screens keep working offline.
  5. Can we get our data out? Ask how exports work before you sign.
  6. Who helps us go live? Ask what onboarding includes and who does the work.
  7. What is the full cost? Ask about setup, training and each module, not only the monthly fee.
