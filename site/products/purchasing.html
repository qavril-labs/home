Questions about this module

What if a supplier bills me for more than I received?

The bill is held until the difference is agreed. You pay only for what arrived.

Can I buy in a foreign currency?

Yes. The bill is booked in your base currency at the rate on the bill date.

How it works

  1. A team member raises a request, and the manager approves it.
  2. The approved request becomes a purchase order for the supplier.
  3. When goods arrive, the receipt is checked against the order before the bill is paid.

A typical example

A merchant orders cement. The delivery is short by ten bags, and the bill is paid only for what actually arrived.
