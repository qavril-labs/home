Questions about this module

Is the bill posted automatically?

No. The system proposes the entry, and a person approves it before it reaches the ledger.

Can I send bills by email?

Yes. Bills sent to your capture address appear in the approval queue.

How it works

  1. Photograph or upload a supplier bill or receipt.
  2. The system reads the supplier, date, totals and lines, and suggests the account for each line.
  3. A person checks the suggestion and approves it, and only then is it posted.

A typical example

A manager photographs a fuel receipt at the end of the day. The entry appears for approval the next morning, with the cost already matched to the vehicle.
