Yes. Invoices and statements go out by email, and message channels are available where your country supports them.

Does it handle tax on each line?

Each line can carry its own tax code, and the totals show tax by rate, as your country's rules require.

How it works

  1. Create a quote and send it to the customer.
  2. Turn the accepted quote into an invoice with the next number in sequence.
  3. Record payments as they arrive, and follow up overdue invoices from the aging list.

A typical example

A consultant bills for a month of work. The invoice is numbered in order, and the customer's late payment appears on the aging list the following week.
