Questions about this module

Can an agent spend money without asking?

No. An agent can only draft. Spending and posting need your approval, unless you set a limit you are comfortable with.

How do I stop an agent?

Switch it off from its page. It stops at once, and its open drafts stay in the queue for you to review.

How it works

  1. You set what each agent may do and how much it may spend or post.
  2. The agent drafts its work, such as a follow-up or a purchase order.
  3. You approve, change or reject it. Every action is logged, and you can stop an agent at once.

A typical example

A restocking agent notices that a fast-selling item will run out in five days. It drafts a purchase order, and the owner approves it with one tap.
