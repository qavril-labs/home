A day with the system

  1. Morning: the stock count is entered from a phone at each outlet, and low items are flagged.
  2. Lunch: sales at the till reduce the stock as they happen, and the kitchen gets each order on screen.
  3. Evening: the owner sees the day's sales for all three outlets on one page.
  4. Month-end: the report is ready the same day, with no re-typing.
