A day with the system

  1. Booking: each reservation holds the room and shows on the calendar at once.
  2. During the stay: bar, kitchen and laundry charges are added to the guest's bill as they happen.
  3. Checkout: one bill is printed with every charge, and the room is marked for cleaning.
  4. Month-end: occupancy and revenue per room are ready without a notebook.
