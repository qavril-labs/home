The problem

Nobody is sure which family owes what until term end. Receipts are handwritten, and reminders are sent from memory.

Modules that would matter

  • Sales and invoicing for fee plans
  • Receivables aging and reminders
  • General ledger with clear receipts

What a demo would show

We would show a family's fee plan, a mobile money payment that clears the balance, and the reminder that goes out before the next installment.

Example scenario. This describes a typical business, not a real customer.

A day with the system

  1. Term start: each family's fee plan is set up once, with its installments.
  2. Payment day: a mobile money payment clears the balance and issues a numbered receipt.
  3. A week before each installment: the family gets a reminder that shows the amount due.
  4. Term end: the bursar sees who owes what, in one list, by class.
