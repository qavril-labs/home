FAQ

Questions we are asked

Short, direct answers. If yours is not here, book a demo and ask it on the call.

Are the AI bots safe to use with real money?

Bots draft and flag. Anything that changes money, stock or a customer needs your approval, and every action is logged so you can review it.

Can I switch a bot off?

Yes. Each bot can be paused or stopped at any time, and it stops at once.

Do I need an accountant to use the ledger?

No. Your accountant can review the books at month-end, and the screens are written in plain words for owners and managers.

What if I already use spreadsheets?

You can keep them while we move your records across. The move is part of onboarding, so you do not do it alone.

How do I know my customers' data is not shared?

Each business's records are kept separate from every other business's, and only people in that business with the right role can see them.

Can my bookkeeper or accountant have access?

Yes. Access is given by role, so your accountant can see the books without seeing everything else.

Can I use it today?

Yes. Book a demo, and we set up your business with you, starting with the modules you need first.

What does a free trial include?

Trial access is not open yet. Requesting one puts you first in line, and we email you when your trial is ready. Nothing is charged for a trial.

How does a demo work?

A demo lasts about 20 minutes. Bring one real task, such as a sale or a month-end report, and we show how it would run in your business.

Which countries are supported?

We launch in the United States, Europe, the Middle East and North Africa (MENA), and Asia. Tax, e-invoicing and payroll rules are set for each country and reviewed by a local expert before they are used, so each country opens when its rules are ready.

Where is my data stored?

Each customer's data is kept separate from every other customer's. The hosting region is confirmed in your written terms.

Can I move my data from QuickBooks or Tally?

Yes. We move your opening balances, customers, suppliers and stock across in a guided setup, and check them against your last closed statements.

Will my staff need training?

Yes. Every team gets a short onboarding session. The screens are designed for people who are not accountants.

Does it work offline?

The till and stock screens keep working when the connection drops, and the sales sync when it returns.

What are AI agents, and do I stay in control?

Agents do routine work inside limits you set. They draft, you approve, every action is logged, and you can stop an agent at any time.

How is pricing decided?

Plans start free. Paid plans are a monthly subscription plus a one-time setup fee, and the exact amount is confirmed in writing before you pay. See the pricing page.
