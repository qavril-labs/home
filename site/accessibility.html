Accessibility

Accessibility statement

We aim to meet WCAG 2.2 level AA for this website. This statement is a draft until an independent audit has checked it.

What we do

  • Every form field has a visible label, and errors are written next to the field and announced to screen readers
  • Motion is reduced for visitors who ask for it, and the 3D scene is replaced by a still layout
  • Pages work at phone width, can be zoomed, and can be used with a keyboard alone
  • Colour contrast is checked in both the light and dark themes

Known limits

The software's own screens are still being audited against the same standard. We will publish the results before the software is generally available.

Report a problem

If something is hard to use, email connect@qavrillabs.com and tell us the page and what happened. We aim to reply within two business days.
