Onboard Ola

New staff setup bot

A new starter is ready on day one, and a leaver's access ends on time.

See Onboard Ola in a demo All bots

What Onboard Ola does

Prepares a new starter's access, equipment and first-week checklist.

Works with

Employee recordsAccess and rolesEquipment list

Features

  • Creates the starter's record and role
  • Assigns the first-week checklist
  • Removes access on the last day
  • Keeps a record of every access change

Functions

Create a starter record
Assign a checklist
Schedule access removal
Remind the manager of open tasks

How a task runs, step by step

  1. Reads the new starter's details from the offer
  2. Creates the record and the right role
  3. Assigns the first-week checklist to the manager
  4. Removes access on the last working day, with a manager's approval

Activity feed

Sample entries that show the kind of record this bot keeps.

  • 08:00New starter Amina joins MondayRecord and role created
  • 08:01First-week checklist assigned to the managerSent for confirmation
  • 12:00Laptop and card on the equipment listMarked as ready
  • 17:00Leaver's access set to end FridayWaiting for manager approval

What you control

  • Choose the role and checklist for each new starter
  • Approve each access change before it is made
  • Set the date when a leaver's access ends

Guardrails

Limit: Access changes need a manager's approval. Every action is logged, and you can pause Onboard Ola at any time.
