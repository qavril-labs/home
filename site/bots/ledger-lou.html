Ledger Lou

Month-end checker

Checks the month before you close it, and points at what needs a look.

See Ledger Lou in a demo All bots

What Ledger Lou does

Prepares reconciliation lists and checks month-end before the books are closed.

Works with

General ledgerBank linesOpen invoices and bills

Features

  • Matches bank lines to open invoices and bills
  • Flags entries posted after a close
  • Builds the month-end checklist
  • Explains each flag in plain words

Functions

Build a reconciliation list
Flag an unusual entry for review
Prepare the close checklist
Compare this month with the last three

How a task runs, step by step

  1. Reads the bank lines for the month
  2. Matches each line to an invoice or bill
  3. Lists what did not match, with the likely reason
  4. Hands the checklist to the accountant

What you control

  • Choose the bank accounts and periods the bot reads
  • Set the tolerance for a matched difference
  • Review the checklist before any period is closed

Guardrails

Limit: Reads and flags. It never closes a period by itself. Every action is logged, and you can pause Ledger Lou at any time.
