Forecast Finn

Cash forecast bot

Shows where your cash is heading, and why, before it gets there.

See Forecast Finn in a demo All bots

What Forecast Finn does

Forecasts cash for the coming weeks, with the assumptions shown so you can change them.

Works with

Bank linesReceivables and payablesPayroll dates

Features

  • Forecasts cash for the coming weeks
  • Shows the assumptions behind each figure
  • Warns when cash drops below your floor
  • Shows what changes the forecast most

Functions

Refresh the forecast each morning
Test a 'what if' on payments
Set a cash floor
Explain the biggest change this week

How a task runs, step by step

  1. Reads incoming and outgoing payments
  2. Adds the known bills and payroll dates
  3. Projects the balance for the next weeks
  4. Warns early if the balance is heading below the floor

Activity feed

Sample entries that show the kind of record this bot keeps.

What you control

  • Set the cash floor that triggers a warning
  • Choose which payments and payroll dates the forecast uses
  • Change assumptions and see the new forecast

Guardrails

Limit: Forecasts are estimates. Assumptions are yours to edit. Every action is logged, and you can pause Forecast Finn at any time.
