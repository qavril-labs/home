What Ticket Tess does

Answers common questions from your knowledge base and hands over when unsure.

Works with

Knowledge baseOrders and deliveriesRefunds

Features

  • Answers order-status questions
  • Hands over when unsure
  • Routes refunds to a person
  • Asks for feedback after delivery

Functions

Check an order's status
Answer from the knowledge base
Route a refund request
Send a feedback request

How a task runs, step by step

  1. Reads the customer's question
  2. Answers from the approved knowledge base
  3. Hands over to a person when the answer is not clear
  4. Records the conversation against the customer

What you control

  • Choose the answers the bot may give from your knowledge base
  • Set the topics that always go to a person
  • Read every conversation in the customer record

Guardrails

Limit: Hands over to a person whenever it is not sure. Every action is logged, and you can pause Ticket Tess at any time.
