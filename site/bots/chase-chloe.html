Chase Chloe

Payment reminder bot

Polite, persistent and never rude. Reminders that get paid.

See Chase Chloe in a demo All bots

What Chase Chloe does

Sends polite reminders for overdue invoices, and escalates to a person when needed.

Works with

ReceivablesCustomer statementsEmail and SMS

Features

  • Reminders that match each customer's history
  • Escalates at 14 and 30 days
  • Creates a call task for the owner
  • Stops when the payment arrives

Functions

Send a reminder at 3 days late
Send a firmer reminder at 14 days
Create a call task at 30 days
Pause reminders for a customer

How a task runs, step by step

  1. Checks each invoice against its due date
  2. Sends the reminder that fits the days overdue
  3. Escalates to a call task when needed
  4. Stops the moment a payment clears the invoice

What you control

  • Set the reminder days, for example 3, 14 and 30
  • Choose the channel: email, SMS or a call task
  • Pause reminders for any customer at once

Guardrails

Limit: Reminders follow the wording you approve. Every action is logged, and you can pause Chase Chloe at any time.
