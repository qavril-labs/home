# Design-partner programme

Status: **draft, not started.** Nobody has been contacted, and no business is named anywhere until it gives written permission.

## Purpose

Test one sentence with real businesses before building more:

> Every number in your books can be traced to the entries behind it, and AI only proposes; a person approves.

The programme answers three questions:

1. Do owners and accountants care about traceable figures enough to change how they work?
2. Does the approval queue fit how their staff already approve things?
3. What would stop them from moving their records over?

## Who we want (3 to 5 businesses)

- A business that keeps books in a spreadsheet, a basic accounting tool, or on paper, and has a monthly close it finds painful.
- One clear owner who makes decisions, and a bookkeeper or accountant who uses the system daily.
- A first market we support: the United States, Europe (including the UK), the Middle East and North Africa, or Asia.
- A willingness to give honest feedback every fortnight, including negative feedback.

## What a partner gets

- Free onboarding and a setup call, at no charge during the programme.
- Direct access to the team building the product.
- A say in the first vertical we finish.
- Pricing agreed in writing before any charge, with the programme terms stated.

## What we ask of a partner

- Two sessions a month: one working session and one feedback call.
- Use the product for one real task (a sales invoice, a payment, a month-end check) for at least eight weeks.
- Written permission for every use of their name, logo or results. Without it, nothing is published.

## What we will not do

- Publish a name, logo, quote or result without written permission.
- Describe a feature to a partner as working when it isn't built.
- Charge a partner for the programme.

## Success measures (agreed with each partner before they start)

- The partner can trace an invoice total to its entries on their own, without help, by the end of week four.
- Approvals take less time than their current process, as the partner measures it.
- The partner says in writing whether they would move their records over, and what would stop them.

## Gates before the programme starts

- [ ] Legal review of the terms and the privacy notice for each partner's region.
- [ ] A named person responsible for partner contact.
- [ ] A feedback log, kept in the repository's docs and reviewed weekly.
