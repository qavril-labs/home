# Expert Network pilot: accounting

Status: **design only.** No expert has been recruited, no project has been commissioned, and no payment terms are agreed. Each step needs a named owner and the reviews listed below.

## Why accounting

Accounting has clear answers, so expert review can be checked against a standard. It is also a domain our own books product already covers, so the pilot tests the process on work we understand.

## Scope of the pilot

- One AI company as the commissioning customer, with written consent before anything is published.
- One kind of work: review of model answers to accounting questions, such as classifying a transaction, reading a trial balance, or explaining a reconciling difference.
- Between 200 and 500 items, in two batches, so quality is checked before the second batch.

## Expert requirements

- A qualified accountant or bookkeeper with a current professional qualification in their jurisdiction. Qualifications are checked against the issuing body's public register before work starts.
- A signed confidentiality agreement and a conflict-of-interest declaration.
- Experience in the jurisdiction the questions cover.

## Quality process

1. Each item is answered by one expert and checked by a second, independent expert.
2. Disagreements go to a senior reviewer, who decides and records the reason.
3. The team re-checks a sample of 10% of agreed items every week.
4. Every item carries its provenance: who reviewed it, when, and under which rule set.

## Governance (in place before the first item)

- Confidentiality agreements with the commissioning customer and with each expert.
- Data-rights and intellectual-property terms agreed in writing.
- No personal data in the items. Items containing personal data are removed before they reach an expert.
- Credential checks recorded, as the expert's jurisdiction requires.

## Measures of success

- The two experts agree on at least 85% of items, with a documented process for the rest.
- The commissioning customer accepts the batch, with written feedback.
- Every item can be traced to its reviewers and rule set.

## Reviews required before the pilot starts

- [ ] Legal review of the commissioning terms, the expert agreement and the data terms.
- [ ] Review of the credential checks for the chosen jurisdiction.
- [ ] A named owner for expert recruitment and one for quality.

## What the pilot does not do

- It is not a self-service marketplace. The team recruits and manages the experts.
- It gives no tax, legal or audit advice to anyone.
- It does not publish an expert's name without that expert's written consent.
