# 1. Executive Summary

QAVRIL LABS is envisioned as a global technology and intelligence company that helps organisations modernise operations, build digital products, adopt artificial intelligence, automate business processes and make better decisions using connected data and intelligent systems.

The company will operate through specialist technology studios, an integrated engineering foundation, a portfolio of proprietary products and a comprehensive enterprise solutions practice.

QAVRIL LABS will not position itself exclusively as:
- An AI development agency.
- A chatbot development company.
- An ERP software vendor.
- A workflow automation consultancy.
- A traditional IT services company.

Instead, QAVRIL LABS will combine these capabilities into a single integrated business.

### 1.1 Core business proposition

**We research, design, engineer and deploy intelligent technology that transforms how businesses operate.**

Our business will have four primary activities:
1. **Consult:** Identify business problems, technology opportunities and transformation priorities.
2. **Build:** Develop software, AI products, agents, enterprise systems and digital experiences.
3. **Automate:** Connect systems, automate workflows and introduce intelligent decision-making.
4. **Operate:** Deploy, monitor, maintain and continuously improve solutions.

### 1.2 Strategic objectives

- Establish QAVRIL LABS as a premium global technology brand.
- Build reusable AI and automation capabilities.
- Develop proprietary SaaS and enterprise software products.
- Deliver custom AI engineering and enterprise transformation projects.
- Create industry-specific solutions rather than generic software alone.
- Establish recurring revenue through subscriptions and managed services.
- Build a technology ecosystem in which products share common infrastructure.

# 10. Technology Architecture

### Architectural principles
- Modular architecture.
- API-first engineering.
- Multi-tenancy with strict tenant isolation.
- Model-agnostic AI architecture.
- Cloud-native deployment.
- Observability by design.
- Secure software development lifecycle.

### Model-agnostic AI runtime
Supports OpenAI, Gemini, Claude, and open-source models (Ollama, vLLM) with unified routing, fallback policies, token metering, and evaluation.

# 11. Security, Privacy & Responsible AI

Every product and service must satisfy:
- Authentication, RBAC, and tenant data isolation.
- Audit trails and append-only financial logs.
- Agent permission sandboxes and tool calling whitelists.
- Cryptographic single-use token approvals for consequential actions.
- Output validation, prompt injection defense, and continuous evaluation.
